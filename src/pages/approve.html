This page uses plain http. Anyone on your network path could see or change it. Only allow this on your own local network.

To catch the redirect reliably, the browser will also ask to let the extension see requests to these sign-in hosts. If you refuse, the sign-in still opens, but a phone-app redirect may be missed; you will not be asked again for them.

    The extension never sees your password. The page receives the final sign-in address (it contains a one-time sign-in code) and nothing else.
